What Jackson gets

Everything below is already built and running on real county, hail and roof data for Collin, Dallas, Denton and Rockwall counties. The link you were sent opens it.

Storm map

Every storm on file, with the homes that took one inch hail or larger lit up street by street. Pick a storm and see where to work.

Daily 50

Each morning, a ranked list of 50 homes to reach first: age of the roof, hail it took, and whether a re-roof permit is already on file.

Turnover Radar

Streets where neighbors have re-roofed lately, so you can walk a block with a reason to knock and a story to tell.

LiDAR measurements

A 3D roof for the home with squares and pitch estimated from LiDAR. It is an estimate, typically within about 10% of permit records, so order a measurement report before you quote.

Homeowner proof page

A simple page for each home showing the storm date, hail size, how close it was reported and what hit the street. You send it with one tap, from your own phone or email.

CRM

Inbox, pipeline and calendar for every home you work, from new hit to signed contract, with the next step always on top.

People stay in charge of every message. Nothing goes to a homeowner unless someone on your team taps send.
